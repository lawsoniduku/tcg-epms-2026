# Migration and reconciliation

1. Obtain approved HRIS and legacy extracts, effective dates, source references, period ownership and control totals. Use an isolated staging database and retain source files in TCG-controlled storage.
2. Create internal manager accounts and map external manager identifiers. Import employees by stable staff ID. Resolve duplicate emails, manager gaps, probation status, commercial role mappings and company scope.
3. Preview historical scores in Migration reconciliation. The application retains raw ERP status, raw score and the observed canonical stage separately. It quarantines unknown staff, invalid scores, duplicate period keys and missing cycles. A supervisor score is evidence of submission. Historical trial records do not become calibrated policy outcomes.
4. Correct and resubmit failed batches. Compare totals by company, department, period and stage with the signed source. Record PM certification and the crosswalk reference.
5. Rehearse the period freeze, backup, restore, representative permissions, score calculations, exports and payroll acknowledgements. Sign off rollout in the designated environment. Retain reconciliation evidence and the rollback decision owner.

# PMS delivery and acceptance plan

The acceptance exercise starts with an empty database. Engineering provisions accounts and a roster, HR and the executive approve a dated policy, and HR opens a cycle. Every score in that cycle must originate from submitted measures or authorised observations.

| Order | Working outcome | Acceptance exercise | Accountable reviewer |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Installation, accounts, scoped roster, working-day calendar, versioned policy and strategy | Start a persistent service, provision separate users, import a roster, approve a policy and open a cycle | IT and HR |
| 2 | Quarterly appraisal | Create linked KPIs, check weights, approve and lock the baseline, record check-ins, self-assess and submit a manager assessment | Employee, manager, HOD and PM |
| 3 | Behaviour evidence and completion | Nominate raters, approve and collect responses, suppress small groups, calibrate, moderate, lock, release, hold a conversation, acknowledge and close | HR and functional heads |
| 4 | Commercial and probation | Validate a commercial month and inspect rolling recovery. Complete probation checkpoints and record an authorised decision | Sales, Finance, managers and HR |
| 5 | Employee outcomes | File an appeal, carry out an independent review, manage a PIP, model merit costs against budget and assess succession readiness | ER, Finance, Executive and Talent |
| 6 | Integration acceptance | Exercise the server from a clean install, verify access denial and persistence, document exports, backup and restoration, publish code and review screens | Engineering and business owners |

Use synthetic records for acceptance. Live dates, BSC perspective weights, commercial schedules, deduction authority, merit configuration and delegations require the appropriate TCG decisions. Each active cycle carries its configuration snapshot. The deployment runbook distinguishes product functionality, site-specific integrations, policy approval and external acceptance. Test evidence determines the status of each item.

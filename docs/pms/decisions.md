# Policy configuration and ownership

| Decision | Owner | Operational treatment |
|---|---|---|
| BSC perspective conflict (D01) | HR and Executive | PM enters the four approved weights. Total must equal 100%. Executive publishes the dated policy. The acceptance example is fictional |
| Working-day timetable (D02) | PM and Executive | Review actual holidays and cycle deadlines. Target defaults are based on period end and working days |
| Merit cadence, rates, caps and budget (D03/D04) | Finance, HR and Executive | Enter an approved window and matrix reference, simulate eligible salary proposals, check equity and obtain authorisation before export |
| Commercial schedules and recovery (D05/D08) | Commercial, Finance and ER | Record approved targets, minimum attainment, quality caps, role rubrics and ramp status. Validate source data independently |
| Behaviour freshness and suppressed cohorts (D06/D11) | HR and Privacy | Approve respondent coverage and the anonymity minimum for the combined independent pool. Each valid external observer contributes once to the same-period score |
| Incomplete periods and scope (D07/D16) | HR, HOD, Finance and Executive | Use a documented eligibility exception or dated track assignment. Missing evidence requires review |
| Delegation, hearing and employment authority (D09/D15) | HR, ER, Legal and Executive | Provision the correct role and record the signed delegation and case clearance. Review corporate separation of duties and company scopes |
| Historical crosswalk and closure (D10/D12) | HRIS and PM | Reconcile by authoritative staff ID. Keep source workflow text separate from supervisor score presence |
| Inflation and salary action components (D13) | Finance and Executive | Use comparable CPI periods in the scenario tool. Distinguish merit, COLA, market correction and promotion components |
| Confirmation contract terms (D14) | HR and ER | Record individual contract/schedule reference. Confirm the legal and contractual basis for extension or non-confirmation |
| Deduction events, authority and limits (D-FIN) | HR and Executive | Disabled in the acceptance policy. A new published policy must expressly enable the mode and specify an allowable cap, score floor and event basis |

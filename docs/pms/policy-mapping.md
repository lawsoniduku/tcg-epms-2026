# Functional acceptance map

| Domain | What can be exercised | Evidence and scope |
|---|---|---|
| Organisation and track allocation | Stable staff-ID roster, linked accounts, company/department scope, confirmed commercial/non-commercial and probation routing, dual approval of prospective mixed-role assignments | Roster HTTP and scope tests. Cycle enrolment and confirmation tests. HRIS remains authoritative |
| Strategy and KPIs | Five linked strategy levels, quantitative targets, direction, approved milestone/lower-is-better anchors, critical gates, weight validation and leadership KPI requirement | Positive approval journey and incorrect-input guards. A prospective amendment preserves the baseline |
| Quarterly cycle | Manager endorsement, PM QA, HOD baseline, check-ins, self-review, overdue bypass, supervisor assessment, panel appointment, calibration, exceptional-rating moderation, finalisation, feedback conversation, receipt and closure | Domain journey and desktop browser walkthrough. Department closure metrics distinguish approved exceptions |
| Behaviour and 360 | Manager nomination, PM validation, confidential observer submission, moderation, category sufficiency and explicitly configured redistribution | Tests exercise three valid peers, suppression at two, missing categories and employee access denial |
| Manual finalisation | Applicable supervisor/calibration score, authorised deductions, explanation and evidence, calculated preview, immutable revisions, controlled reopen and release | Finalisation regression suite and integrated browser review. CSV preview/apply shares the same calculation |
| Commercial | Monthly targets, role-schedule approval, weekly coaching, source freeze/import, Finance validation, quality gate, manager review, recovery stages, acknowledgement and correction | Automated monthly recovery and CSV provenance tests. Formal quarterly reviews exclude commercial staff |
| Confirmation | Plan approval, months 1/3/5, month 6 assessment, role-specific weights, ER review, human decision, one ordinary extension, prospective routing | Automated checkpoint-to-confirmation journey |
| Employee protections | Alternate reviewer, exception approval, receipt, appeal, independent appointment, review and reassessment; preserved prior revisions | Appeal and immutable-history tests. Late appeals require a stated reason |
| Capability and conduct | Coaching, HOD escalation, ER diagnostic/warning, 30/60/90-day PIP, support, HOD activation, checkpoints, employee representations, hearing, authority and separate investigation/suspension route | Automated support-to-hearing test. Legal clearance is referenced and remains subject to corporate verification |
| Rewards | Recognition, distinct merit windows, pay snapshots, eligibility exceptions, CPI comparisons, proposed increases, pool scaling, HR equity review, executive approval, separate Finance exporter and acknowledgement | Budget, salary scenario and reconciliation tests. Payroll execution belongs to the receiving system |
| Talent | Sustained performance history, separate potential evidence, 9-box display, readiness, succession nomination, executive decision and development note | Automated nomination and access tests. Commercial nominations use validated monthly history and a signed common-rating mapping reference |
| Operations | Dashboards, scoped completion/score CSVs, local or SSO sign-in, account deactivation, HRIS API, notification outbox, audit and restore | HTTP authentication, CSRF, company scope, replay rejection and backup/restore tests |
| Migration and cutover | Source status and score kept separately, ID/cycle duplicate checks, quarantine, sign-off, multi-role rollout approval and production startup gate | Automated reconciliation and approval-sequence test. Real ERP history and attachments require mapping in TCG staging |

## Items for acceptance and further implementation

The tests exercise representative paths. The supplier has not certified every detailed acceptance item in the PRD. Review high-consequence decisions with TCG's approvers before live use. The standalone release includes quarantined evidence attachments with a scanner integration, role-specific commercial component weights and recurrence review, and multiple granted roles per account. Validate these against the corporate file service, commercial schedules and delegation matrix. The hosted role-switching workspace exercises fictional users; individual corporate accounts belong to the standalone installation. Benchmark the database adapter against the expected TCG population. Complete a full device, accessibility, load, privacy and penetration review in staging.
